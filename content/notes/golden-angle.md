Seeds in a sunflower head are arranged so that each new one sits about **137.5°** around from the previous one — the golden angle. Because that angle is "maximally irrational", seeds never line up in straight spokes, and they pack with very little wasted space.

The drawing on the homepage of this site is generated from exactly that rule: point *n* sits at angle *n × 137.5°* and distance proportional to *√n* from the centre.
