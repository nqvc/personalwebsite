Sunflowers track the sun with no motors and no sensors — growth on one side of the stem does the work. Some engineered trackers are described as "passive", using materials that expand when heated.

Question worth exploring: what would the trade-offs be compared with sensor-based tracking? Precision, speed, cost, maintenance?
