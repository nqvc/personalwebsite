Two rules that make almost every circuit problem solvable:

1. **Current law (KCL):** the current flowing into a junction equals the current flowing out. Charge doesn't pile up anywhere.
2. **Voltage law (KVL):** around any closed loop, the voltage rises and drops add up to zero. Energy per charge is conserved.

What helps me: KCL is bookkeeping for *stuff* (charge), KVL is bookkeeping for *energy*.
