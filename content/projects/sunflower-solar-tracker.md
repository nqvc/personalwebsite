## Background and problem

A fixed solar panel only faces the sun directly for a short part of the day. For the rest of the day light arrives at an angle and less of it is captured. Tracking systems try to fix that by moving the panel — but they add cost, moving parts and energy use of their own.

## Motivation and initial idea

Young sunflowers do something similar without any electronics: they turn through the day to follow the sun. This project started from a simple question — *could a tracker borrow that behaviour?*

## Limitations and possible improvements

Some limitations apply to almost any small tracking prototype and are worth testing explicitly:

- Energy used by the tracking mechanism itself, which reduces the net gain.
- Behaviour on cloudy or overcast days, when light is diffuse.
- Durability of moving parts over long periods outdoors.
