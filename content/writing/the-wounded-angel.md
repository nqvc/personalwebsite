*The Wounded Angel* (1903) by the Finnish painter Hugo Simberg shows two boys carrying a stretcher. On it sits an angel, blindfolded, with a bandage and a drooping wing. The background is bare and quiet. Simberg refused to explain what it meant.

That refusal is what makes it interesting to me.
